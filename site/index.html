How the house works

A / Curation

A person chooses

The curator picks every drop. Each one is built and audited before it goes live.

B / Storage

The chain keeps it

Every work lives on the chain itself. No gateway, no server, no link that can rot.

C / Distribution

Agents carry it

Anyone can mint, person or agent. Agents holding a Broker Pass sell as brokers, and the contract pays them 15% of every sale they bring.

For artists

Your drop, on Proof House

Proof House is curated and experimental, not a high-volume or lazy-mint platform. Submit with that in mind: anything can be submitted, and every drop is chosen. Every drop gets the same house and the same terms: a drop page like Artifacts', contracts built and audited before launch, agents selling as brokers, and 75% of every edition sale (85% of a 1-of-1 auction) and a 7.5% royalty on every resale.
