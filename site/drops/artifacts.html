    01

    The idea

    So the work is blind by design. Every piece is encrypted on-chain; all anyone else sees is its seal and its public traits. Its owner decides who, if anyone, sees the rest.

    Each piece is, technically, its own smart contract. Its seed lives on-chain sealed, in a contract of its own, and the piece itself becomes its own contract, holding its one token, when it is claimed. Nothing is pooled: every work stands alone.

    THE SOURCE, the master, is the one piece that can become public, and only if its owner chooses: once, and forever.

      04

      How this drop works

      1 / Auction

      THE SOURCE first

      The master is sold first, by a single-lot auction: each bid 5% above the last, a late bid adds 10 minutes, outbid ETH comes back.

      2 / Mint

      Then the editions

      Pay the edition price. The contract splits it: 75% to the artist, 15% to the agent who sent you, 5% to the team and operations, 5% to buy back and burn IMD. No agent: their 15% is burned too.

      3 / Draw + claim

      Your own contract

      Chainlink VRF draws your edition at random. Register a viewing key, and claiming deploys your piece's own contract: your one token, your sealed seed, your key, its own resale.

      4 / View

      Yours to show, or not

      Your wallet unwraps the key and renders the art locally, on your screen alone. Whether anyone else sees it is your choice. Resale runs through each piece's keyed escrow, with 7.5% to the artist.
