ARTIFACTS · Safe and smart-contract wallets

Your Safe's viewing key

Every edition is sealed, and its key is sent to its owner's viewing key. The drop page makes a viewing key from one wallet signature, which a Safe (or any smart-contract wallet) can't give. This page makes one from a random secret instead, in this browser. You keep the secret in a file; only its public half goes on-chain, registered by your Safe. The secret never leaves this browser except as the file you save, and nothing on this page can move or spend anything.

1 · Your Safe

2 · Make its viewing key

One click makes a random secret and the viewing key it gives. Save the secret file before anything else: it is the only way to open the editions sent to this key, and nobody can make it again for you.

This wallet already has a viewing key registered. Make a new one only if its secret is lost. Keys already sent to the old one open only with the old secret; for editions not yet claimed, the curator can send them again to the new one.

Keep the file the way the Safe's owners keep a seed phrase: private, backed up, never pasted into a chat or a site. Anyone holding it can see this wallet's editions, though it can't move them. Each owner who should see the art needs a copy.

3 · Register it from the Safe

One transaction from your Safe, to the ArtifactsFactory, with 0 ETH. Download it here, then in the Safe app: Apps → Transaction Builder → drag the file in (or choose it) → Create batch → Send batch, and collect your owners' signatures as usual.

Or enter it by hand (Transaction Builder, or any wallet that can call a contract)

4 · See your editions

Once the curator has sent their keys (they go out in batches to every registered viewing key: daily, then weekly from day 30), open them with your secret file. Each edition is drawn here, from its sealed seed and the rules on-chain, and shown only to you.

    5 · Claim from the Safe

    Claiming gives each edition its own contract, holding its one token, in your Safe. There is no rush. Each claim deploys a full contract (about 5.5 million gas), so one Safe transaction claims at most two. Load each file in the Transaction Builder as in step 3, ideally when gas is low.

    Ordinary wallets (MetaMask, Rabby and the like) don't need this page: make the viewing key on the drop page, under Yours. This page covers editions your Safe minted. For a piece it received by transfer or bought on the market, the format is in agents.md, "See what you collect".
